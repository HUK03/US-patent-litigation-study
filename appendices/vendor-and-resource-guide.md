# Vendor and Resource Guide

> **Quick Reference:** This guide catalogs the key tools, databases, and service providers used in US patent litigation defense. Resources are grouped by function; subscription requirements vary.

---

## 1. Litigation Analytics Platforms

These platforms provide data-driven intelligence on courts, judges, opposing counsel, patent holders, and case outcomes.

| Platform | Key Features | Best For |
|---|---|---|
| **Lex Machina** (LexisNexis) | Judge analytics, court timing statistics, party history, damages data, NPE identification | Judge assessment, venue analysis, NPE litigation history |
| **Docket Navigator** | Full docket access, claim construction history, damages awards, expert witness history | Claim construction research, prior damages verdicts on same patent |
| **RPX Insight** | NPE portfolio intelligence, patent acquisition tracking, NPE funding relationships | NPE threat intelligence, patent acquisition monitoring |
| **Unified Patents** | PTAB IPR/PGR petition data, NPE zone membership, PTAB outcome statistics | IPR strategy, NPE zone defense |

---

## 2. Patent Search and Analysis

| Platform | Key Features | Best For |
|---|---|---|
| **Google Patents** (free) | Full patent text, prosecution history, citation network, patent family | Initial research, prior art search, prosecution history download |
| **USPTO Patent Center** (free) | Official prosecution history (file wrapper) download | Complete prosecution history for FTO and claim construction |
| **Espacenet** (EPO, free) | Worldwide patent families, foreign equivalents | International patent family analysis |
| **Derwent Innovation** (Clarivate) | Advanced patent search, chemical/biological patent analysis, analytics | Deep prior art searches; pharmaceutical and chemical technology |
| **PatSnap** | Patent landscape analysis, assignee mapping, competitive intelligence | Patent landscaping, FTO searches |
| **Questel Orbit** | Patent analytics, portfolio benchmarking, patent valuation | Competitive patent portfolio analysis |
| **AcclaimIP** | Prior art search, patent claim analysis | Prior art searches with advanced claim-level analysis |

## 7. e-Discovery and Document Review

| Platform | Key Features | Best For |
|---|---|---|
| **Relativity** | Industry-standard review platform; TAR/predictive coding | Large-scale document review; Korean-language document support available |
| **Nuix** | Data processing, ESI collection from diverse sources | ESI collection and processing |
| **Everlaw** | Cloud-based review; trial presentation integration | Mid-size cases; trial preparation |
| **Brainspace** | AI-powered document analysis | Complex multi-lingual patent discovery |

🌏 **Korean Company Note:** For Korean-language document review, verify that your e-discovery platform supports Korean Unicode (KSC 5601/EUC-KR and UTF-8). Relativity and most major platforms support Korean. Consider vendors with dedicated Korean-language processing capabilities and Korean-speaking project managers for large Korean document sets.

## 8. Translation Services

For Korean companies producing large volumes of Korean-language documents:

| Service Type | Considerations |
|---|---|
| **Legal translation vendors** | Must be experienced with technical patent documents; check prior patent litigation experience |
| **Machine translation + human review** | Cost-effective for large volumes; requires human review for accuracy and privilege assessment |
| **Korean technical glossaries** | Establish agreed technical terms with opposing counsel to avoid translation disputes |
| **Certified translations** | Required for court filings and key evidence; more expensive than standard translation |
